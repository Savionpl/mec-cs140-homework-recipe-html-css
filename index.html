My Favorite Recipe: Homemade Brownies

Brownies are one of my favorite desserts because they are chocolatey, soft, and delicious. This recipe shows you how to make homemade brownies from scratch!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a baking pan or line it with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the butter in a bowl. Add the sugar and cocoa powder and mix everything together.
  3. Add the eggs and vanilla extract. Stir until the mixture is smooth.
  4. Add the flour and salt. Mix until everything is combined, but do not overmix the batter.
  5. Pour the brownie batter into the prepared baking pan and spread it evenly.
  6. Bake for about 20–25 minutes. Check with a toothpick; a few moist crumbs are fine.
  7. Let the brownies cool before cutting them into squares. Enjoy your homemade brownies!
